Pros of Agile Development:
1. Agile allows teams to respond quickly to changing requirements, making it easier to adapt to new information or stakeholder feedback.
2. Frequent iterations and reviews improve collaboration and visibility, allowing issues to be identified and addressed earlier.

Cons of Agile Development:
1. Agile can be difficult to manage for large teams or projects due to frequent communication and coordination requirements.
2. The lack of a fixed long-term plan can make cost and timeline estimation less predictable compared to non-Agile approaches.







---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Scrum Master is responsible for ensuring the Scrum process is followed correctly and helping the team work effectively. This includes removing impediments, facilitating Scrum ceremonies, and coaching the team on Agile and Scrum practices.

The Product Owner is responsible for maximizing the value of the product. This includes defining and prioritizing the product backlog, clarifying requirements, and representing stakeholder and customer interests to the development team.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Sprint Planning:
   - The team discusses which backlog items will be included in the sprint.
   - The scope and goals of the sprint are defined, and tasks are estimated.

2. Daily Scrum:
   - Team members share what they worked on previously and what they plan to work on next.
   - Any blockers or issues preventing progress are identified.

3. Sprint Review:
   - The team demonstrates completed work to stakeholders.
   - Feedback is collected, and the product backlog may be updated based on that feedback.
