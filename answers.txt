Pros of Agile Development:
1. Agile allows teams to respond quickly to changing requirements, making it easier to adapt to new information or stakeholder feedback.
2. Frequent iterations and reviews improve collaboration and visibility, allowing issues to be identified and addressed earlier.

Cons of Agile Development:
1. Agile can be difficult to manage for large teams or projects due to frequent communication and coordination requirements.
2. The lack of a fixed long-term plan can make cost and timeline estimation less predictable compared to non-Agile approaches.
